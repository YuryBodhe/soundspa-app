### Music engine

Current hybrid progressive-network plus Blob-cache behavior:

1. A network-backed `HTMLAudioElement` provides progressive HTTPS playback.
2. Startup waits for 5 contiguous buffered media-seconds.
3. Public `playing` requires genuine `currentTime` progression.
4. Buffer health is measured from the buffered range containing `currentTime`.
5. Audible network traffic has priority over full-track fetching.
6. Complete tracks become Blob URLs in two reusable in-memory A/B slots.
7. `desiredNextIndex` stays independent from the Blob currently used as fallback.
8. Fetch lifecycle uses AbortController, timeout, request/generation validation, retry backoff, and URL cleanup.
9. Pause preserves position and cached Blobs; since `76dc20f`, authorized A/B preparation may continue while paused.
10. A complete same-track Blob is preferred when progressive playback becomes unusable.
11. Without a usable Blob, a confirmed-dead network Audio is recreated, the intended position restored, startup buffering repeated, and playback resumed automatically.

Divnitsa URLs:

- `/music/divnitsa/divnitsa-mix-01.mp3`
- `/music/divnitsa/divnitsa-mix-02.mp3`
- `/music/divnitsa/divnitsa-mix-03.mp3`

`Relax` and `432 Hz` remain UI-only.

### Critical recovery invariant

The following are **not** definitive recovery success:

- `audio.buffered` availability or growth;
- `canplay`;
- `playing` event;
- resolved `audio.play()` Promise.

An exhausted Audio may retain/grow buffered ranges while remaining frozen. Recovery succeeds only after observable genuine `currentTime` progression.

## 8. Stable decisions and limitations

Stable decisions:

1. Progressive audio starts before the full music Blob is ready.
2. Five buffered media-seconds gate startup; this is not a fixed wall-clock delay.
3. Genuine `currentTime` movement defines playback/recovery success.
4. Audible traffic outranks cache traffic.
5. Music uses two reusable Blob slots; ambient uses selected-track LRU capped at three.
6. Logical playlist target stays separate from audible fallback.
7. Complete local media outranks silence while testing network recovery.
8. Confirmed-dead network Audio is recreated.
9. `navigator.onLine` is only an acceleration hint.
10. Production isolation is non-negotiable.

Known limitations/deferred work:

- Blob caches are memory-only and disappear on reload/restart.
- Two ~30 MB music Blobs plus a replacement may create a significant memory peak.
- Broader Safari/long-duration coverage remains useful, but there is no open verified exhaustion bug.
- No dedicated offline/recovery UI status exists yet.
- Device auth, entitlements, subscriptions, protected delivery, and persistent offline storage are deferred.
- No Service Worker, IndexedDB, MediaSource, HLS/DASH, crossfade, DRM, or heartbeat has been added.
